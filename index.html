Sime Darby Property University Hackathon 2026

Borrow from next door, not from a store.

TownLoop lets verified residents lend tools, share spaces and find help from neighbours on their own street. Everyone signs in with SDP PRIME, so every account is a real resident from day one and the money spent stays in the township.

Township of Tomorrow Mobile app Landing page Pilot: Elmina Valley

01

How it works

One QR poster per clubhouse. Residents scan, get verified, and start lending within minutes.

1

Scan at the clubhouse

Siti scans the poster, signs in with SDP PRIME and her address is checked on the spot.

2

List or find something

She lists her jet washer at RM30 a day. Tan, two streets away, books it for Saturday.

3

Hand over at the gate

Tan scans Siti's QR code. His RM100 deposit is held and the rental starts.

4

Give it back

Scanned back on Sunday, the deposit is released and both of them earn Eco Points.

02

User flow
