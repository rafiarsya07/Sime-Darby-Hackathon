How it works

  1. 1

    Browse or post

    Tan browses his street, or posts what he needs: a lawn mowed, a party blazer, an aircond service.

  2. 2

    Request, then accept

    Pak Rahman requests to chat. Tan checks his profile, ratings and past jobs before saying yes.

  3. 3

    WhatsApp opens

    Only after Tan accepts does TownLoop introduce them on WhatsApp. Numbers stay hidden until then.

  4. 4

    Close the loop

    Items change hands with a QR scan. 24 hours after the job is done, Eco points land for both.

Lending your things shouldn't feel risky.

  • Real residents only

    Every account is checked against SDP PRIME. No anonymous profiles.

  • No cold messages

    Nobody can chat with you until you accept their request. You see who they are first.

  • Deposit held first

    The borrower's deposit is held before handover. Either side can report a problem within 24 hours.

  • A township safety net

    1.5% of every fee goes into a community fund for repairs bigger than the deposit.
